## 📌 Project Overview

This Power BI project analyses customer purchasing behaviour, marketing campaign performance, product sales, and buyer demographics for **Market Mindz**.

The dashboard transforms customer and transaction data into an interactive analytical tool that helps identify high-performing campaigns, valuable product categories, preferred purchasing channels, and customer segments.

The analysis is presented through two main dashboard views:

- **Campaign Performance**
- **Buyer Composition**

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The dashboard was developed to:

- Evaluate the performance of six marketing campaigns.
- Compare sales and purchases generated by each campaign.
- Identify the highest-performing product categories.
- Analyse customer purchasing behaviour across different sales channels.
- Understand customer demographics and household composition.
- Explore purchasing preferences across different age groups.
- Support data-driven marketing and customer segmentation decisions.

---

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

### 1️⃣ Campaign Performance

This page provides an overview of marketing campaign effectiveness and product performance.

**Key analysis includes:**

- Purchases generated by each marketing campaign
- Total sales by campaign
- Sales contribution by product category
- Purchase quantities across Store, Web, Catalog, and Deal channels
- Product-level revenue performance
- Comparison of campaign performance across purchasing platforms

The dashboard shows that **Campaign 6 generated the highest number of purchases (334)** and approximately **£0.33M in sales**.

At product level, **Wine generated approximately £681K**, followed by **Meat at £374K**, making them the strongest revenue-generating product categories.

---

### 2️⃣ Buyer Composition

This page explores the demographic and behavioural characteristics of the customer base.

**Key analysis includes:**

- Customer distribution by education
- Customer distribution by marital status
- Household composition based on children and teenagers at home
- Average customer income across campaigns
- Purchasing preferences across age groups
- Purchase activity across different sales channels

The dataset contains **2,240 customers** with an overall **average income of approximately £52.2K**.

Store purchases represent the largest purchasing channel, followed by Web, Catalog, and Deal purchases.

---

## 🔍 Key Insights

- **Campaign 6** achieved the strongest overall performance with **334 purchases** and approximately **£0.33M in sales**.
- **Wine** was the highest revenue-generating product category at approximately **£681K**.
- **Meat** was the second-largest contributor with approximately **£374K** in sales.
- **Store purchases** were the most common purchasing method, with approximately **13K purchases**.
- The customer base consists of **2,240 customers** with an average income of approximately **£52,247**.
- Graduates represent the largest educational segment within the customer base.
- Customer purchasing preferences vary across age groups, providing opportunities for more targeted marketing strategies.
